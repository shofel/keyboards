2Thumbs · phone touchscreen · revised research only

I lowered outer-column use; this revised candidate replaces the first proposal.

I incorporated lateral-column discomfort.

The revised candidate uses the outer screen-edge columns for 3.11% of held-out letters and spaces, versus 7.87% in my first candidate and 6.45% in shipped v24. Complete modeled thumb travel is 13.5% below v24.

I interpreted “lateral” as the two outside screen-edge columns.

That means global columns0 and9, not the columns beside the center gap. This interpretation is explicit but unconfirmed. The discomfort is user-provided; the numerical penalty is an assumption. This is not a speed, accuracy or comfort claim, and I made no phone or application changes.

  5. Preserve layer access and distinguish the draft

    Current v24 left NUM/SYM tap-toggle under200ms or hold/chord momentary. Right Return/Space ordinary taps stay ordinary; holds open SYM/NUM with native timeout and Space-swipe arbitration. Alphabet Shift stays. Right-side NUM is reachable while left NUM is held; left-side symbols while right Return is held. Same-side input may need a latched layer; one thumb cannot hold one cell and tap another simultaneously.

    The unfinished draft discusses Dvorak apostrophe, double-space period, removing letter digit popups, NUM punctuation and QWERTY replacement; Shift movement/meaning is tentative. None is treated as implemented or decided here. Punctuation, Shift, layer-use frequency and actual popup travel/time are outside this Russian-letter-and-space comparison, not silently zero-cost experiences. Popup hold cost is the same abstract additive term for all three because incidence is identical.

    You should see: an alphabet research revision only; no new app, firmware or device behavior.

  6. Keep the next decision small

    The smallest useful check is whether the uncomfortable column really means the two outer screen edges modeled here. Until clarified, this is an assumption-labeled candidate, not permission to implement it. The existing owner context can resolve that without another repeated request. Learning cost is high and no global optimum or personal-corpus fit is claimed.
